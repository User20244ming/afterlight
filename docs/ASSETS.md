# Asset provenance

The sample photograph is an original image generated with the built-in image_gen tool during this project. It depicts a seaside cinema at blue hour. The supplied JPEG is `dist/assets/cinema.jpg`. Three H.264 sample videos are made from this same image with different camera crops and motion. They are labeled generated samples in the app. They are examples of the workflow and make no claim to be filmed documentary footage.

The generation prompt was as follows.

> Create a photorealistic cinematic 16:9 photograph for a filmmaking demo called Afterlight. A solitary, weathered seaside cinema building on a deserted coastal road at blue hour, its warm amber entrance lights glowing, fog drifting from the sea, distant dark mountains, wet asphalt reflecting muted neon, very wide composition with a foreground road curving toward the building, old 35mm film grain, subtle anamorphic bloom, deep slate blue, muted orange and cream. Thoughtful quiet composition, shot on 35mm, editorial art photography, no people, absolutely no lettering, logos, words, numbers or watermarks. Detailed real architecture and atmospheric depth. The full image should be one continuous photograph.

DM Sans and Instrument Serif are distributed through Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Their files and licenses are bundled in `dist/assets`. Font sources are https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif. License source files are from the respective directories in https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl.

The interface is authored in HTML and CSS. It does not contain third-party logos, music or stock-footage clips.
